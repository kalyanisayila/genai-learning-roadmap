# Week 3 - Day 19: Role and Persona Prompting

## What is Role Prompting?

Role prompting is a technique where you assign a specific role to the AI before asking a question. This helps the AI generate responses that match the knowledge and style of that role.

### Example

Prompt:
You are a Python developer. Explain Python lists to a beginner.

Output:
Python lists are ordered collections used to store multiple items. They are mutable, meaning you can add, remove, or modify elements.

---

## What is Persona-Based Prompting?

Persona prompting asks the AI to respond with a particular personality, tone, or communication style.

### Example

Prompt:
Act as a friendly teacher and explain Artificial Intelligence to a 10-year-old.

Output:
Artificial Intelligence is like teaching a computer to learn and make decisions, just like humans learn from experience.


## Benefits

- Produces more relevant responses.
- Improves clarity and communication.
- Adapts the response to different audiences.
- Useful for education, coding, customer support, and content writing.


## Key Takeaways

- Role prompting assigns a professional role.
- Persona prompting defines the communication style.
- Both techniques improve AI response quality.

## Conclusion

Role and persona prompting help AI generate responses that are more accurate, engaging, and tailored to the user's needs.
