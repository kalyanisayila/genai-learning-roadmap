# Week 3 - Day 18: Prompt Engineering Best Practices

## What are Prompt Engineering Best Practices?

Prompt engineering best practices are guidelines for writing prompts that help AI models produce more accurate, relevant, and consistent responses.

## Best Practices

### 1. Be Clear and Specific
Write clear instructions and avoid ambiguity.

**Example:**
Summarize the following article in 100 words.

---

### 2. Provide Context
Include background information so the AI understands the task.

**Example:**
You are a software engineer. Explain Python decorators to a beginner.

---

### 3. Define the Output Format
Specify how you want the response.

**Example:**
Return the answer as a table with three columns: Feature, Advantage, and Example.

---

### 4. Break Complex Tasks into Steps
Split difficult tasks into smaller parts.

**Example:**
Step 1: Analyze the problem.
Step 2: Suggest a solution.
Step 3: Explain the implementation.

---

### 5. Use Examples When Needed
Provide one or more examples for better accuracy.

---

### 6. Iterate and Improve
Modify your prompt if the first response is not satisfactory.

## Key Takeaways

- Write clear and specific prompts.
- Provide enough context.
- Define the expected output format.
- Break complex tasks into smaller steps.
- Improve prompts through iteration.

## Conclusion

Well-designed prompts improve the quality, accuracy, and usefulness of AI-generated responses.
